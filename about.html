Our Journey

At the heart of Curry Wala is the culinary vision of Chef Ketan Patel, whose journey has been guided by one simple belief: great food begins with uncompromising quality, authenticity, and care. Chef Ketan's passion for cooking started early, rooted in a deep appreciation for fresh ingredients, hygienic preparation, and the comforting flavors that bring people together. His pursuit of excellence took him across the world, where he refined his skills through professional culinary training in Switzerland, England, and Canada. These international experiences shaped his distinctive approach to Indian cuisine—one that honors tradition while embracing the highest global standards.
In 2014, Chef Ketan brought this vision to life with the launch of Taj Bistro, which quickly became a beloved destination for authentic Indian cuisine and warm hospitality. Building on that success, he expanded his culinary footprint to New York City, where Curry Wala earned a loyal following for its rich flavors, fresh ingredients, and consistently exceptional dining experience.
Now, Curry Wala is excited to open its doors in New Jersey, bringing the same dedication to authenticity, quality, and heartfelt hospitality to a new community. Every dish at Curry Wala is a celebration of India's vibrant culinary heritage—crafted with passion, served with pride, and designed to make every guest feel right at home. Whether you are joining us for a quick meal or a special gathering, we invite you to experience the warmth, flavor, and tradition that define Curry Wala.
