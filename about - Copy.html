I.

Our Story

At the heart of Curry Wala is the culinary vision of Chef Ketan Patel, whose journey has always been guided by one simple belief — great food begins with authenticity, uncompromising quality, and genuine care.
Chef Ketan’s passion for cooking started early, rooted in a deep appreciation for fresh ingredients, hygienic preparation, and the comforting flavors that bring people together. His pursuit of excellence led him across the world, where he refined his craft through professional culinary training in Switzerland, England, and Canada.
In 2014, Chef Ketan brought this vision to life with the launch of Taj Bistro, a restaurant that quickly became known for authentic Indian cuisine and warm hospitality. Building on that success, Curry Wala expanded to New York City, earning a loyal following for its rich flavors, fresh ingredients, and consistently memorable dining experience.
Great food is more than flavor — it is comfort, culture, and connection shared around a table.
Chef Ketan Patel
Founder of Curry Wala

III

Our Culinary
Philosophy

1.
Heritage First
Family recipes and regional classics cooked with respect, not trends. We honor origin while keeping flavors clear and honest.
2.
Spice, Not Just Heat
Whole spices are toasted and ground in-house, layered for aroma, depth, and balance. Heat is optional; harmony is essential.
3.
Fire & Time
Tandoor flame, slow braises, and breads made to order. Patience unlocks texture, sweetness, and that quiet, lingering finish.
4.
Sourced with Care
Seasonal produce, responsible meats, local dairy where possible. Small-batch masalas, minimal waste, maximum integrity.
